Quick FAQ

What file types do you accept?

STL, OBJ, and 3MF are supported right now.

Can you help me choose material?

Yes. Tell us how the part will be used and we will recommend the best option.

Do you do very small orders?

Absolutely — one-off prints are a big part of what we do.

Can I pick up locally?

Yes, choose local pickup in the order form and shipping is removed.
