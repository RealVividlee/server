PLA Variants

PLA has the biggest style range—great when visual character matters as much as function.

Matte PLA

Clean, low-glare surface ideal for modern product looks and architectural mockups.

Silk PLA

Glossy, eye-catching finish for gifts, display parts, and showpieces.

Wood PLA

Natural, wood-like aesthetic for props and decor accents.

Glow PLA

Charges under light and glows in dark environments for novelty or safety markers.
