TPU Variants

TPU variants let you tune flexibility and feel for soft-touch and impact-resistant applications.

95A TPU

Balanced flexibility for bumpers, grips, and protective skins.

85A TPU

Softer and more bendable for wearable comfort parts and seals.

High-Flow TPU

Optimized for cleaner extrusion and smoother flexible walls.

Translucent TPU

Flexible plus light-diffusing, useful for custom protective covers.
