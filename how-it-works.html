How ordering works

1

Share your idea

Upload an STL/OBJ/3MF file (or even a sketch/photo) and tell us what the part needs to do.

2

We suggest the setup

We explain materials and quality in plain English, then recommend the best setup for your goal and budget.

3

Approve and print

You approve the estimate, and we send progress updates until pickup or shipping.
