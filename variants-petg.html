PETG Variants

PETG variants balance durability and style while keeping functional performance front and center.

Carbon Fiber PETG

Stiffer feel and technical appearance for rugged functional components.

Transparent PETG

Light-passing finish for diffusers, guards, and translucent housings.

Matte PETG

Subdued finish for clean utility parts without glossy highlights.

Recycled PETG

Eco-conscious option for prototyping and low-waste projects.
