Materials Lab

Use this quick guide to choose the best filament/resin for your part before you request a quote.

PLA

Best for prototypes, decorative parts, and low-heat indoor use.

  • Strength: Moderate
  • Heat: Low
  • Finish: Smooth and easy to print

PETG

Great all-around durability with better heat and moisture resistance.

  • Strength: High
  • Heat: Medium-high
  • Finish: Slightly glossy, tough

ABS

Good for functional parts that handle higher temperatures.

  • Strength: High
  • Heat: High
  • Finish: Durable, workshop-ready

TPU

Flexible material for grips, bumpers, and shock-absorbing parts.

  • Strength: Flexible-strong
  • Heat: Medium
  • Finish: Soft-touch feel

Resin

Best for ultra-detailed display pieces and precision visual parts.

  • Strength: Varies by resin type
  • Heat: Medium
  • Finish: Highest detail
