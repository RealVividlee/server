Materials Lab

Meet each material like a unique character, then choose the one that best matches your part's personality and purpose.

The Friendly Rookie

PLA

PLA is your easygoing starter hero: clean prints, quick turnaround, and great looks for prototypes and display pieces.

  • Strength: Moderate
  • Heat: Low
  • Best scenes: Prototypes, display models, fit checks

The Reliable Workhorse

PETG

PETG is the practical all-rounder: durable, moisture-tolerant, and usually our go-to for real-world functional parts.

  • Strength: High
  • Heat: Medium-high
  • Best scenes: Outdoor parts, brackets, everyday use

The Tough Veteran

ABS

ABS is the rugged classic: better for higher-heat conditions and hard-use parts that need to take a beating.

  • Strength: High
  • Heat: High
  • Best scenes: Workshop parts, enclosures, warm environments

The Agile Defender

TPU

TPU is the flexible specialist: shock-absorbing, bendy, and ideal when your part needs grip or cushioning.

  • Strength: Flexible-strong
  • Heat: Medium
  • Best scenes: Grips, bumpers, wearable or protective parts
